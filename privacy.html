Privacy policy

Last updated 28 August 2026

The short version: this site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, embeds no third-party scripts, and has no forms. I do not collect anything about you when you read it.

1 · Who is responsible

Indra Kumari Bhattarai, an independent product designer and sole proprietor, of Thimi-Biruwa Road, Anantalingeshwar, Madhyapur Thimi 44800, Bagmati Province, Nepal. Contact: ecomishanbusiness@gmail.com.

2 · What this site does not do

  • No cookies are set by this site.
  • No analytics, tag manager, pixel or session-recording tool is loaded.
  • No advertising or tracking networks are used.
  • No contact form exists, so nothing is submitted or stored.
  • Fonts and images are served from this site itself, not from a third-party CDN, so no request about you is made to another company as you browse.

3 · Nothing is stored on your device

This site writes nothing to your browser — no cookies, no local storage, no session storage. Close the tab and no trace of your visit remains on your machine.

4 · Hosting logs

This site is served by a third-party static host. Like any web server, the host may keep short-lived technical logs — typically IP address, timestamp, requested page and browser user-agent — for security and to keep the service running. I do not have access to those logs, do not use them, and cannot link them to you. Their handling is governed by the host’s own privacy policy.

5 · If you email me

If you write to me, I receive your email address, your name if you give it, and whatever you choose to tell me. I use that only to reply and, if we work together, to run the project. I keep correspondence and project records for as long as the engagement and any legal or tax obligation requires, and I do not sell, rent or share it for marketing. Where a project needs it, I sign a separate confidentiality agreement.

6 · Links away from here

Links to client products or external sites take you to services I do not control. Once you leave, this policy no longer applies; please read theirs.

7 · Your rights

Because I hold nothing about you unless you email me, there is usually nothing to access or erase. If you have written to me and want your correspondence deleted, ask and I will delete it, subject to any records I must keep by law.

8 · Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Since the site collects nothing, changes are likely to be minor.
