I’m Indra Kumari Bhattarai, an independent product designer based in Madhyapur Thimi, in the Kathmandu Valley. I work with SaaS and software teams worldwide.

A landing page is seen once; a product is used daily. The second job is harder, and it is the one I take — dashboards, composers, editors, settings, the screen someone opens for the fortieth time this week.

I work on a few projects at a time, and hand over tokens and reasoning rather than flat files, so a team can keep going without me.
